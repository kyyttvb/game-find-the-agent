    GRAMMAR TIP: MODALS OF DEDUCTION

    MUST = 99% sure it is TRUE

    Use must when the evidence proves something is true.

    "She has blonde hair, glasses, and a wrist tattoo. It must be Agent Viper!"

    MIGHT / COULD = 50% POSSIBLE

    Use might or could when something is possible, but you are not 100% sure yet.

    "Both Shadow and Viper lied. It might be Shadow, or it could be Viper."

    CAN'T = 99% sure it is IMPOSSIBLE

    Use can't when the evidence proves something is impossible.

    "Agent Nova was in London at 22:00. It can't be her!"
